## 🧠 Understanding Email Structure

Think of an email address like a Pakistani postal address:
- **Username** = Your house number and street (must exist)
- **@ symbol** = "at" or "located in" (separator)
- **Domain** = Your city name (like "gmail" or "yahoo")
- **.com/.pk** = Your province/country code

## 🔍 What Makes an Email Valid?

Let's think like a post office worker checking addresses:
1. Must have exactly one @ symbol (not zero, not two)
2. Must have text before @ (your username)
3. Must have text after @ (domain name)
4. After @, must have a dot somewhere
5. Must have text after the dot (like .com, .pk)

## 💭 Breaking Down the Problem

Before writing code, plan your approach:
1. First, check if string contains @
2. Then check if there's text before and after @
3. Then check for dot in the domain part
4. Finally, check text after the dot
